🍗 Chicken Biryani

🧾 Ingredients

  • Chicken - 500g
  • Basmati Rice - 2 cups
  • Onion - 2
  • Tomato - 2
  • Yogurt - 1/2 cup
  • Oil - 4 tbsp
  • Spices - 1 tbsp

👨‍🍳 Steps

1. Fry onions 🧅
2. Add chicken 🍗
3. Add spices 🌶️
4. Cook rice 🍚
5. Mix & steam 🔥

💡 Tips

Use low flame for best aroma 🌿
