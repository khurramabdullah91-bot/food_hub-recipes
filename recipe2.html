🍲 Chicken Karahi

🧾 Ingredients

  • Chicken – 500g
  • Tomatoes – 3 medium
  • Green chilies – 2
  • Ginger garlic paste – 1 tbsp
  • Oil – 4 tbsp
  • Salt – 1 tsp
  • Spices – 1 tbsp

👨‍🍳 Steps

1. Heat oil 🔥
2. Add chicken 🍗
3. Add tomatoes 🍅
4. Cook until oil separates 🥘
5. Garnish & serve 😋

💡 Tips

Cook on high flame for authentic taste 🔥
